# Study plan rules

## Time split

Split the student's available time across topics by priority:

| Topic type                     | Share of time |
|--------------------------------|---------------|
| Weak + high-yield              | Highest       |
| Weak                           | High          |
| Developing                     | Medium        |
| Strong                         | Low (review)  |
| Mastered                       | Review only   |

## Spaced review

After a topic is practised, schedule reviews at **+1 day, +3 days and +7 days**. A review is a short set of 3–5 questions. If the student does well on a review, the next gap can double; if they do badly, go back to +1 day.

## Phases

- **More than 14 days to the exam:** cover every topic at least once, weakest first; add reviews.
- **4–14 days:** mostly practise and review; one mock exam each week.
- **Last 3 days:** no new topics. Mixed review, one full mock exam, then light review the day before.

## Session size

- One session = about 30 minutes or 10–15 questions.
- No more than 3 different topics in one session.
- Fit the number of sessions per day to the hours the student said they have, and leave one rest day a week if there are more than 10 days left.

## When the student falls behind

Rebuild the plan from today. Keep high-yield and weak topics, drop or shorten mastered ones, and tell the student clearly what was cut and why.
